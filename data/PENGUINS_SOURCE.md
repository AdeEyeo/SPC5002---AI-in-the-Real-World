# penguins_raw.csv

The file holds field measurements of adult nesting Adélie, Chinstrap and Gentoo penguins on three islands in the Palmer Archipelago, Antarctica, across the 2007-08, 2008-09 and 2009-10 seasons, and it is real data that has not been generated, tidied or prepared for this module.

Dr Kristen Gorman and the Palmer Station Long Term Ecological Research programme collected it, and the programme is part of the US Long Term Ecological Research Network.

Before egg laying began in each season, the team chose and marked study nests that had a pair of adults, and when a nest reached the one-egg stage both adults were caught, measured, weighed and blood-sampled. The blood gave the sex, determined by PCR in a laboratory, together with the two isotope ratios, and the methods section of the paper describes the whole procedure in full.

The paper is Gorman KB, Williams TD and Fraser WR (2014), Ecological sexual dimorphism and environmental variability within a community of Antarctic penguins (genus Pygoscelis), PLoS ONE 9(3), e90081.

The data is released under the CC0 1.0 public domain dedication, so you may use it for anything, but you should cite it anyway.

The file was retrieved from the `palmerpenguins` R package repository, `allisonhorst/palmerpenguins`, under the path `inst/extdata/penguins_raw.csv`.

The raw file is used here on purpose, because a cleaned version of this dataset is in wide circulation, with shorter column names, no `Comments` column and the isotope measurements dropped. That version is easier to work with, but its interesting part has been removed, and the `Comments` column is what brings this file into the module.
