# penguins_raw.csv

Real data, not generated, not tidied and not prepared for this module.

It holds field measurements of adult nesting Adélie, Chinstrap and Gentoo
penguins on three islands in the Palmer Archipelago, Antarctica, across the
2007-08, 2008-09 and 2009-10 seasons.

Dr Kristen Gorman and the Palmer Station Long Term Ecological Research programme
collected it. The programme is part of the US Long Term Ecological Research
Network.

Each season the team chose and marked study nests that had a pair of adults,
before egg laying began. When a nest reached the one-egg stage, both adults were
caught, measured, weighed and blood-sampled. The blood gave the sex, by PCR in a
laboratory, and the two isotope ratios. The methods section of the paper
describes it in full.

The paper is Gorman KB, Williams TD and Fraser WR (2014), Ecological sexual
dimorphism and environmental variability within a community of Antarctic
penguins (genus Pygoscelis), PLoS ONE 9(3), e90081.

It is released under the CC0 1.0 public domain dedication, so you may use it for
anything. Cite it anyway.

It was retrieved from the `palmerpenguins` R package repository,
`allisonhorst/palmerpenguins`, file `inst/extdata/penguins_raw.csv`.

This is the raw file and not the tidy one on purpose. A cleaned version of this
dataset is in wide circulation, with shorter column names, no `Comments` column
and the isotope measurements dropped. It is easier to work with and it has had
the interesting part removed. The `Comments` column is the reason this file is
here.
